**Syndrome datasets (`syndromes/`)** are simulated by this project (CC BY 4.0). The noise models behind them

CC BY 4.0); the all-cat and Kerr-cat models use the operation models of Le Régent et al., Quantum 7, 1198 (2023)
and Darmawan et al., PRX Quantum 2, 030345 (2021). Please cite them together with this project.

**Exception — `digitized/`:** these files contain numbers extracted from figures of
third-party publications (currently: Figs. 2c, 4b, 7, 12, 15d, 16, 18b, 18c, 31, 36 and 37d of Putterman et al.,
Nature 638, 927 (2025), arXiv:2409.13025; Fig. 7b of Putterman et al., Phys. Rev. X 15, 011070 (2025),
arXiv:2409.17556, published under CC BY 4.0). They are provided for scientific reproducibility
only; credit and any rights belong to the original authors, and they are not re-licensed by
this project. Always cite the original publication when using them. The same applies to the
columns of `validation/` files that copy such numbers or are computed only from them (columns
named `data_*`); the model columns of those files are ours (CC BY 4.0). In
`validation/logical_bitflip.csv` the `total_*` columns combine our model with a data-derived idle
part and carry the same attribution requirement.

**Derived from the public Ocelot dataset:** the `data_*` columns of `validation/zenodo_*.csv`, `validation/syndrome_phase.csv`, `validation/logical_bitflip.csv` (rows with data_source = zenodo_raw_fit), `validation/phase_logical_model.csv` and `validation/logical_total.csv`, and all columns of `validation/zenodo_phase_logical.csv` and `validation/zenodo_phase_runs.csv` (the raw syndromes decoded with our DEM), are computed from the raw data of
